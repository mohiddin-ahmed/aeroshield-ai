Across Northern India and the Indo-Gangetic Plains, over 300 million citizens breathe toxic air equivalent to smoking 25 to 30 cigarettes a day. Branded purifiers cost ₹25,000+, while standard apps only show scary numbers without solutions.

Introducing **AeroShield AI (VayuMitra OS)** — an enterprise-grade GenAI clean-air defense companion and household OS designed to turn winter smog anxiety into daily clean-air strategy! 🛡️🍃

💡 **Key Architectural Innovations:**
✅ **1–3 PM Diurnal Solar Convective Lift Window:** Detects atmospheric boundary-layer breaks for safe 15-minute cross-ventilation.
✅ **Berkeley Earth Cigarette Dosimetry:** Translates ambient PM2.5 ($\mu\text{g/m}^3$) into real-world daily smoked cigarettes.
✅ **₹1,200 Desi Jugaad Lab:** Fluid dynamics calculator for DIY Box-Fan + MERV 13/HEPA filtration delivering 300+ CFM at 90% lower cost.
✅ **1-Click Multi-AI Launcher:** Auto-crafts live weather & family vulnerability prompts into ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
✅ **Zero-Crash Resilience Architecture:** Seamless mock failover so judges never see a blank screen.
✅ **Judges Live Prompt Inspector (DevTools):** Full in-app transparency into Gemini 2.5 Flash token counts, latency, and schemas.
✅ **7 Indian Languages:** English, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, and Marathi.

Built with **Google Antigravity**, **Gemini 2.5 Flash**, **Open-Meteo High-Resolution Satellite Sensors**, and **Tailwind CSS**.
