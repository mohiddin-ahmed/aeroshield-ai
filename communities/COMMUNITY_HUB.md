## 4. How to Talk & Engage with Other Developers

When networking or posting in these channels, use these 3 high-impact templates:

### A. Requesting Peer Feedback (Discord / Reddit):
> *"Hey builders! Working on the PromptWars Virtual Challenge. Just deployed our intent-driven app with zero-crash fallback and a live prompt inspector. Would love 2 minutes of your thoughts on the UI and latency before final submission: [Link]"*

### B. Asking Technical Clarifications:
> *"Hey everyone, quick question regarding the Gemini 2.5 structured output schema for the event planner challenge: Has anyone experienced rate limits when chaining 3 prompts? Here is how we resolved it with a mock fallback engine [Link to blog]. How did you tackle it?"*

### C. Build-in-Public Check-In (LinkedIn / Twitter/X):
> *"Late to register, but never late to execute. Diving into Challenge 5 of #PromptWars Virtual. Here is a sneak peek of our prompt architecture running on Google Antigravity. Let's build! 🚀"*
