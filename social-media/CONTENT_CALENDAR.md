# 📅 PromptWars Social Media Strategy & Content Calendar

> **Objective:** Build strong public visibility, engage the Hack2skill and Google for Developers jury, gain developer followers, and position yourself as a top-tier vibe coder.

---

## Content Pillars
1. **The Builder Narrative (30%):** Authentic updates on starting late, overcoming bugs, prompt debugging, and personal growth.
2. **Technical Showcase (40%):** Demos of working apps, prompt architecture breakdowns, Mermaid diagrams, and live videos.
3. **Developer Tips & Insights (20%):** Lessons learned, prompt engineering patterns, avoiding rate limits, and vibe coding techniques.
4. **Community Engagement (10%):** Polls, questions, tagging organizers, and celebrating fellow participants.
