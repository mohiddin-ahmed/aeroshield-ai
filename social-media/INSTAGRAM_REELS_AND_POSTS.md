## 🎨 2. Aesthetic Instagram Carousel Templates (5-Slide Format)

### Carousel: "5 Deadliest Mistakes to Avoid in AI Hackathons"
* **Slide 1 (Hook):** Big bold text on dark background: *"5 Mistakes Developers Make in AI Hackathons (And How to Fix Them)"* + PromptWars badge.
* **Slide 2:** *Mistake #1: The Private Repo Trap.* If judges can't view your GitHub without logging in, it's an instant 0. Always test in Incognito.
* **Slide 3:** *Mistake #2: The Blank Screen Crash.* Your free API key runs out of quota when judges test it. Fix: Build a Zero-Crash Mock Fallback!
* **Slide 4:** *Mistake #3: Ignoring the Tech Blog.* 25% of points come from your write-up. Don't write 3 lines; provide architecture diagrams and prompt logs.
* **Slide 5 (CTA):** *Save this post for your next hackathon! Follow along as I build for PromptWars Virtual. 🚀*
