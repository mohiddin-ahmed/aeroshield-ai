## ⚡ Post 2: Challenge 2 — Real-Time Decision Support (CrisisGrid AI)

**Hook:**  
In high-stakes emergencies, a generic AI answer is as dangerous as no answer at all. 🚨  

For **Challenge 2** of **PromptWars Virtual**, I wanted to tackle something real: **Emergency Resource Routing during Natural Disasters**.  

Introducing **CrisisGrid AI**—an intent-driven decision intelligence engine built on **Google Antigravity**.  

When disasters strike, emergency teams deal with rapidly shifting variables: flooded roads, power outages, and overwhelmed hospital beds.  

Instead of a single speculative answer, CrisisGrid generates **3 Risk-Stratified Decision Pathways**:  
1️⃣ **Conservative:** Maximum survivability with verified safe overland routes.  
2️⃣ **Balanced:** Optimal dispatch combining mobile medical vans with boat transports.  
3️⃣ **High-Speed Contingency:** Immediate air-drop coordinates with fallback shelters.  

Equipped with our in-app **Live Prompt Inspector**, judges and operators can audit the exact token constraints and system instructions powering every decision in real time.
